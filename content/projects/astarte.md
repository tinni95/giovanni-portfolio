# Astarte - Artist Social Media Platform

A specialized social media platform designed exclusively for artists to showcase their work, connect with fans, and monetize their creativity.

## Project Overview

Astarte is a comprehensive social media platform that empowers artists to build their online presence, share their creative journey, and connect with a global community of art enthusiasts.

## Key Features

- **Portfolio Showcase**: High-quality image and video galleries
- **Live Streaming**: Real-time art creation sessions
- **Commission System**: Direct artist-client communication
- **NFT Integration**: Blockchain-based art ownership
- **Community Features**: Forums, challenges, and collaborations

## Technologies Used

- **Frontend**: React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, GraphQL, MongoDB
- **Real-time**: Socket.io, WebRTC
- **Blockchain**: Ethereum, Web3.js
- **Media**: AWS S3, CloudFront CDN
